    About this timeline

    Each star is a scholar, placed in the lane of their main discipline at the middle of their life. The bars at the top are the debates they argued over, spanning the years each debate was most active. Faint threads join each debate to the scholars who took part in it.

    Select a scholar to see the places of their life on the globe, in order. Select a debate to follow where it was argued, year by year. With nothing selected, the globe shows where the scholars in view lived, and turns as you move through the centuries.

    Positions in each debate are stated as their holders stated them. Dates are shown in both the Common Era and the Hijri calendar; a “c.” marks an approximate year, and Hijri years worked out from Common Era dates are marked the same way.

    What still needs work

    This is a first version with 99 scholars and 18 debates. The dates come from general reference works and still need checking against specialist sources, and references are not yet attached to each entry. Ibadi and Isma’ili scholars, Southeast Asia, Africa beyond Sokoto and more women scholars are still to be added.

    The map

    Region names on the globe are historical: Mawara’ al-nahr, Khurasan, al-Andalus. Coastlines, rivers and lakes are modern, from Natural Earth (public domain), except the Aral Sea, which is drawn at its historical extent. City positions are approximate.

    Keyboard

    Tab to any star or debate and press Enter to open it. Use the arrow keys in the timeline to move through time, and Escape to close a panel.
