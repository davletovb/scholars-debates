# Editorial status

Every scholar, debate and event lists its sources in `src/data-sources.js`, shown at the end of its panel.

## Audits and revisions

- **November 2026 accuracy audit** — Checked birth years, teacher links, works and several itineraries and dated events against specialist reference works (TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, Encyclopaedia Iranica, the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy and others), and added sources for every scholar, debate and event.
- **November 2026 full-content review** — Narrowed several position cards and biographies, added death-date notes where sources disagree, corrected map sites and routes (Siffin, Ulugh Beg, Ali Qushji, al-Nawawi and Suhraward), upgraded secondary citations to specialist reference works and peer-reviewed studies, closed internal debate gaps (`tafsir`, `reform`, `renewal`, `philosophy`, `sufism-law`, `heavens`, `sama` and `ijtihad`), and linked five previously unlinked scholars into existing debates.

## Current coverage

The dataset currently holds 98 scholars, 20 debates and 29 events across seven eras and 108 places.

- **1500–1800 era**: 10 scholars counted by life midpoint (the rule used in the Browse modal), or 13 with overlapping lifespans.
- **Sciences lane**: 7 scholars counted by primary lane, or 8 including secondary `also` tags.
